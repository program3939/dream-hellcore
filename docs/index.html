The story

Every room is a Hellstage, built from its own number. Each one is larger, stranger and darker than the last. You never die. The dark just takes you back.

The descent

It starts as a dream.

Hellstage 1 is a quiet indigo night with an aurora overhead and stars under the path. Within ten rooms the color drains away. By Hellstage 30 the lights die behind you. Nothing is built by hand: every room grows from its number, and the same number always grows the same room.

  1. Hellstage 1

    Dream

    Fireflies, a music box, a sea of stars beneath the path.

  2. Hellstage 19

    Dread

    Embers below. Eyes in the walls that turn to follow you.

  3. Hellstage 60

    Terror

    A flash of lightning shows the crowd that was always there.

  4. Hellstage 100,000

    The void

    Rooms the size of cities. Geometry that should not exist.

Mechanics

Every few rooms, a new rule.

The goal never changes: reach the next checkpoint. The way there does. Every jump is checked against the physics before a room is built, so the path is always fair, even when it no longer looks like it.

A director that never repeats

Tension rises in waves and breaks at every checkpoint. The director never fires the same event twice in a row. It goes silent before the worst moments, and sometimes the scare you are waiting for never comes.

Sound made while you play

No music loop and no audio files. Wind, a heartbeat, breathing behind you, whispers at one ear, a choir in which one voice keeps sinking a quarter tone. Every sound is generated live.

Some gates open upward

The False Heaven

An ivory hall in golden light. A choir in perfect rows, every face blank, one figure far too tall. It is the most beautiful place in the game. When lightning strikes, the mask slips, and for a moment the hall is black and red.

Escape rooms

Ten locked gates.

Every fourth Hellstage ends in a puzzle chamber. There is no text on the walls. Plates, pins, lights and tones explain each rule, and you answer by stepping on plates. Mistakes never kill you, but the room answers back.

  1. Hellstage3Lights Out

    Each plate flips itself and its four neighbours. Make every plate glow.

  2. Hellstage7Scale

    Every plate sings. Step on them from the lowest tone to the highest.

  3. Hellstage11The Odd One

    Every pillar has a twin, except one. Stand before it.

  4. Hellstage15Sum

    Light the plates whose values add up to the number at the gate.

  5. Hellstage19Mirror Image

    Copy a pattern of light as its mirror image.

  6. Hellstage23Every Stone Once

    Step on every plate exactly once, always onto a neighbour.

  7. Hellstage27Counting Lock

    Count the figures in the room and set each counter to match.

  8. Hellstage31Counter

    One plate counts up, one counts down. Stop exactly at the mark.

  9. Hellstage35Secret Code

    Guess the code. The gate shows how many symbols are in place.

  10. Hellstage39Coupled Wheels

    Each plate turns two counters at once. Fill them all.

Stuck? Press H and the next correct plate lights up. Or open the pause menu with Esc and skip the puzzle.

After the first ten, the puzzles rotate and grow. Hellstages 9, 17, 25 and on are trials instead: stand still, run from the dark, or do not look.

Install

Nothing to install. Unzip and start. Windows may warn about an unsigned app: choose More info, then Run anyway.

System

Windows 10 or 11, or Linux, 64-bit. A graphics card with OpenGL 3.3. Headphones recommended.
